# Licensing and third-party notices

The repository currently contains a root GNU GPL v3 license and includes legacy/third-party source material. A repository-wide replacement with MIT could incorrectly relicense code whose copyright or upstream terms do not permit it.

## License files
- `LICENSE`: existing GNU GPL v3 terms; retained until every included component's provenance and licensing are audited.
- `LICENSE-MIT`: MIT terms for original ArkansasIo contributions only, where the contributors hold the necessary rights and where this does not conflict with third-party obligations.
- Third-party code, map data, models, textures, fonts, and other assets remain subject to their own licenses and attribution requirements.

## Before changing the root license
Audit source directories and asset metadata, identify copyright holders and upstream license files, preserve required notices, and obtain permission where required. Do not assume generated content or a code rewrite removes obligations attached to copied source or assets.

Copyright year for the new MIT notice: 2026.
